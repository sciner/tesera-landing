Interface, Style and Comfort in Every Detail

  • Seamless design from menus to gameplay: Intuitive UI elements, accessible even to newcomers, with no rough or unfinished parts.

  • Real-time information: See creature health, block durability, and other essential data at a glance.

Post-Release

The Universe Grows With You!

The release isn’t the end - it’s the beginning of a new chapter. The game’s universe will continue to grow and evolve based on your feedback. Expect strange new layers, dreamlike cities, and game-changing mods that reshape reality itself.

Add to wishlist

New Layers & Biomes – Every Step, a Challenge

  • Aquatic Layer: Build underwater bases, battle leviathans, and uncover sunken cities lost beneath the waves.
  • Zero-Gravity Layer: Even your blocks float (probably). Construct orbital stations and chase drifting loot through space.
  • Realm of Darkness: No light, no rules. Light becomes a strategic resource, and every step in the shadows is a fight for survival.

Multiple Characters – One Account, a Thousand Stories

  • Create alt-characters with unique roles: Engineer, builder, explorer - your world, your path.
  • Each character retains individual progress: Inventory, reputation, and world impact are saved separately.
  • Shared account inventory: Cosmetics and collectibles are available to all characters.
  • Custom naming: Give each character their own name - or keep them all under one identity. Your call.

Settlements – When One House Isn't Enough

  • Advanced simulation: Residents work, rest, rebel, and react to your decisions. Influence the economy or plunge the town into chaos.
  • City Builder Mode: From cozy decorations to functional buildings - control every aspect of your settlement to reap long-term rewards.

Bots & Automation – Laziness Drives Innovation

  • Programmable bots: Code routines for mining, repairing, or even trading - and watch them do your work for you.
  • Share your logic: Upload your bot scripts to the workshop so others can join in on the laziness.
  • Mega-automation: Weapon factories, AI-powered farms, fully autonomous bases. Your outpost becomes a living, breathing machine - just like you.

Mini-Apps – Games Within the Game

  • Useful and fun terminals: From calculators to actual in-game apps, players can create and share utilities or games via floppy disks or the workshop.
  • Arcade Machines: Take a break from survival and climb the leaderboard in competitive minigames.

Global Mods – Infinity Is Just the Beginning

  • Developer-level mod tools: Change physics, add new dimensions, write your own campaigns - turn the game into your own universe builder.
  • Full Steam Workshop support: Experience total conversion mods that reinvent the game from the ground up.
  • Extended creator API: If you’ve got a great idea, we’ll support you. Expand the world of Tesera together.

Tesera is a sci-fi sandbox game in a mysterious alien world. Explore an endless voxel world, defend yourself against aggressive creatures and drones, building your dream settlement alone or with friends.
